About Me

I am currently a senior project associate in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology(IIT) Kharagpur, working with Dr. Monosij Maitra. Previously, I was a visiting fellow at Advanced Computing and Microelectronics Units, Indian Statistical Institute (ISI), Kolkata, under the supervision of Prof. Sourav Chakraborty. I am grateful to the ACMU community for their warm welcome and support during my time at ISI Kolkata. I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Sciences at the National Institute of Science Education and Research (NISER), Bhubaneswar, under the supervision of Prof. Deepak Kumar Dalai.

My research interests broadly lie in cryptography and algebraic methods, with particular focus on theoretical aspects of symmetric key cryptography and related problems arising in the design and analysis of cryptographic constructions. Currently, I am exploring the design of zero-knowledge-friendly symmetric primitives.

Research Projects

  • Boolean Functions: Construction and analysis of cryptographically significant Boolean functions.
  • Permutation Polynomials: Algebraic and computational study of permutation mappings over finite fields.
  • Cryptographic S-Boxes: Study of nonlinear vectorial Boolean functions for symmetric-key cryptography.
  • ZKP / MPC / FHE: Exploring algebraic constructions and symmetric primitives suitable for privacy-preserving computation.
